# Ecom Express Sales & Revenue Dashboard

## Project Overview
The Ecom Express Sales & Revenue Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze and monitor e-commerce sales performance, revenue trends, order insights, and cancellation metrics. This dashboard helps businesses understand product performance, customer purchasing trends, and region-wise revenue distribution through visually appealing reports and KPIs.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights, enabling better decision-making and performance tracking.

---

## Key Features
- Total Revenue Analysis
- Average Sales Monitoring
- Cancellation Rate Tracking
- Lost Revenue due to Order Cancellation
- Total Orders Overview
- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue by Product Name
- State-wise Revenue Analysis
- Quarterly Revenue Trend Analysis
- Interactive Filters & Slicers

---

## Dashboard Insights
- Identified top-performing product categories and products.
- Analyzed states generating the highest revenue.
- Tracked quarterly sales trends and revenue fluctuations.
- Monitored cancellation percentage and business losses.
- Compared revenue contributions across different categories.
